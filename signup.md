# Sign up.

Release news, early access, tips and tricks. Email only; we confirm by email; unsubscribe in one click. [Privacy →](https://dozersandbox.com/privacy.md) [Read past issues →](https://dozersandbox.com/blog/index.md)

## How to sign up

The sign-up form needs a browser. Choose one:

- Open <https://dozersandbox.com/signup> in a browser, enter an email address and tick at least one topic. The same form is at the end of the home page.
- Or, in Terminal: `doz signup`.

We send a confirmation email first; nothing else is sent until you click its link. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. See the [privacy policy](https://dozersandbox.com/privacy.md).
