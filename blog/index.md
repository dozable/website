# Blog

News, early access and tips and tricks. Every post is also sent by email to the people who asked for that kind of news. [Atom feed](https://dozersandbox.com/blog/feed.xml) · [Sign up](https://dozersandbox.com/signup.md)

- [OpenCode 2 is in Dozer Sandbox](https://dozersandbox.com/blog/opencode-2.md) — 17 October 2026 — Tool news: OpenCode 2, a rebuild of the open-source coding agent around one shared background service, now runs in Dozer Sandbox beside OpenCode 1, on your own key or OpenCode Zen's free models.
- [Grok Build now tells Dozer what it's doing](https://dozersandbox.com/blog/grok-build-status.md) — 17 October 2026 — Tool news: Grok Build 1.0.52 reports when it's working, waiting for you or done, and Dozer Sandbox 0.35 shows it in the dashboard, as it does for Claude Code and pi.
- [Dozer Sandbox 0.35 - try 42 AI tools in one command](https://dozersandbox.com/blog/dozer-0-35.md) — 17 October 2026 — Release news: Dozer Sandbox 0.35 runs 42 coding agents and AI tools, each one command away, on your own key from any major provider or through a gateway, with a guide beside every try.
- [Introducing Dozer Sandbox](https://dozersandbox.com/blog/introducing-dozer-sandbox.md) — 11 October 2026 — Release news: Fast, resumable, secure Linux sandboxes for AI coding agents on your Mac — free and open source. Here's what it is, why it exists, and how to try it in a minute.
- [Dozer Sandbox 0.33 - see what your agent is doing](https://dozersandbox.com/blog/dozer-0-33.md) — 11 October 2026 — Release news: Dozer Sandbox 0.33 shows what each agent is doing at a glance, upgrades with one command, runs the dashboard in the background, and ends setup with an optional sign-up.
